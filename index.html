Lighter version focused on gamble / paper style rigs. Smaller file, does what it needs to do.

Download Gamble Rig

What these do

Both mods are pretty lightweight and meant for DonutSMP. They’re mainly used when setting up or running rigs on the minigames.

Fake Loot/Ely is the one that spawns the fake items (elytra, diamond blocks, etc). Gamble Rig is a simpler version that some people prefer for certain setups.

Jambles Paper Game Arrow Game Other games

Features

Fake loot

Elytra, diamond blocks and other items for testing setups.

Light on resources

Doesn’t tank your fps while the rig is running.

Simple install

Just drop the jar in your mods folder with Fabric.

Made for DonutSMP

Built around the games that are actually on the server.
